📺 Show on the TV

The TV page (3×3 cameras, red banner, three radar loops) just opened. Tap Go full screen there, then send it to the TV:

iPhone / iPad (AirPlay): Control Center → Screen Mirroring → your Vizio.

Android: quick settings → Cast (or Chrome ⋮ → Cast…) → your Vizio.

Mac / PC: Chrome ⋮ → Cast, save and share → Cast… → Sources: Cast tab. Or Control Center → Screen Mirroring on a Mac.

Roku TV (e.g. Hisense): the Chrome Cast menu will not work. Use AirPlay: on the TV turn on Settings → Apple AirPlay and HomeKit, then Screen Mirroring from a Mac or iPhone. On Android or Windows use Miracast (TV: Settings → System → Screen mirroring → Prompt).

Both Vizio SmartCast (Chromecast built in) and AirPlay work for free. The phone or computer and the TV must be on the same Wi-Fi, and the phone has to stay on and awake while it is showing. Apple does not let a web page cast to a TV any other way, so mirroring is the only option for iPhones.
